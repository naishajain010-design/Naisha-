Classification guide (PCT)

● LOW < 0.5 ng/mL – sepsis unlikely, low risk
● MID 0.5 – 2 ng/mL – sepsis possible, moderate risk; re-test in 6–24 h
● HIGH ≥ 2 ng/mL – sepsis likely, high risk (≥ 10: severe sepsis / septic shock likely)

Research prototype, not a diagnostic device. Uncalibrated values are only rough estimates. Keep lighting and camera the same for every photo, and confirm with a validated lab test and clinical assessment.
